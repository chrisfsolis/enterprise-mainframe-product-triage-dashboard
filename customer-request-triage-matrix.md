# Customer Request Triage Matrix (Fictional)

| Criteria | Definition | Scale | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Renewal / Retention Risk | Likelihood request impacts renewal confidence | 1 (low) to 5 (high) | 20% |
| Regulatory / Audit Impact | Compliance or audit exposure if not addressed | 1 to 5 | 20% |
| Customer Severity | Operational pain and urgency | 1 to 5 | 15% |
| Revenue Impact | Financial exposure or expansion influence | 1 to 5 | 10% |
| Engineering Effort | Delivery complexity (inverse in scoring) | 1 to 5 | 10% |
| Support Burden Reduction | Reduction in repeated support incidents | 1 to 5 | 10% |
| Modernization Value | Alignment with migration/transformation outcomes | 1 to 5 | 5% |
| Adoption Potential | Expected increase in feature/product usage | 1 to 5 | 5% |
| Strategic Fit | Alignment with product direction | 1 to 5 | 3% |
| Customers Affected | Breadth of likely impact across accounts | 1 to 5 | 2% |

## Priority band guidance
- **P0:** Critical continuity/compliance/retention risk; immediate action recommended
- **P1:** High-value and near-term roadmap candidate
- **P2:** Important but can sequence after top risk/value items
- **P3:** Monitor or defer pending stronger signal
