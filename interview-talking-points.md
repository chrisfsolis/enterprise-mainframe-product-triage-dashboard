# Interview Talking Points

## 30-second explanation
I built the Enterprise Mainframe Product Triage Dashboard as a portfolio prototype to demonstrate practical PM decision-making for mature enterprise software. It uses fictional customer requests and a transparent scoring model to show how I balance renewal risk, regulatory impact, customer severity, engineering effort, and modernization value without overcomplicating execution.

## 60-second explanation
This artifact is designed to show product judgment, not engineering complexity. I framed a common enterprise PM problem: many customer requests arrive through disconnected channels, but teams still need consistent prioritization. I created a static triage dashboard with fictional requests, risk-focused scoring, and explicit PM recommendations. The interface highlights P0/P1 items, regulatory and renewal-sensitive work, and modernization opportunities that preserve operational continuity. I also added a backlog outline, scoring model, and interview narrative so a recruiter or hiring manager can understand my roadmap thinking quickly, even without a live walkthrough.
